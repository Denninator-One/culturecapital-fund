01 — Thesis

Operators
and Builders

A strong startup can raise capital from anyone, but what truly matters is who stands behind them and what comes with the investment. We are operators who have built companies across media, real estate, finance, and technology. We invest early because we know what early looks like and what it takes to grow a vision into something real. We are always on the lookout for operators who are already getting traction, and ready to redefine the categories they're entering. We back the founder just as much as the product.

Seed-Stage, In Market

We invest at seed and Series A in companies that have already proven they can develop a viable product and generate market traction.

Cross-Portfolio Pollination

Every company in our portfolio has been introduced to the others. We treat the portfolio as a network, not a spreadsheet; actively engineering collaboration where it creates value.

Experience In Your Corner

Our team has built and scaled companies across multiple industries. We bring pattern recognition from the trenches, not just the term sheet.
